This simple beanie works up quickly in soft, baby-safe cotton yarn, making it a great last-minute gift or baby shower project. Sized for 0-6 months; adjust rounds for other sizes.

## Materials

- Soft DK weight cotton yarn (about 40g)
- 3.5mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle
- Stitch marker

## Abbreviations

- **MR** — magic ring
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Sizing Guide

- Newborn: work through round 6, then straight rounds until 4 inches tall
- 0-3 months: work through round 7, then straight rounds until 4.5 inches tall
- 3-6 months: work through round 8, then straight rounds until 5 inches tall

## Pattern

1. MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. (1 sc, inc) around (18)
4. (2 sc, inc) around (24)
5. (3 sc, inc) around (30)
6. (4 sc, inc) around (36)
7. (5 sc, inc) around (42) — for 0-3 months and up
8. (6 sc, inc) around (48) — for 3-6 months

Once you reach the appropriate stitch count for your size, stop increasing and work even rounds of sc without further shaping until the hat reaches the desired height (see sizing guide above).

## Brim (optional)

For a folded brim look, work the last 1-2 inches in front-post/back-post double crochet ribbing instead of plain sc, which naturally curls into a snug, stretchy fold.

## Finishing

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Weave in all ends. Add a small crocheted flower, bow, or pom-pom to the top or side if desired.

*Tip: baby heads grow fast — if gifting for a future date rather than a newborn now, size up one category to allow room to grow.*

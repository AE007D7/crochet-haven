This little black cat is worked in simple pieces and assembled at the end, with an optional arched-back pose for extra Halloween spookiness. A sweet, quick decoration for the season.

## Materials

- Black worsted weight yarn (about 30g)
- Small amount of green or yellow yarn for the eyes
- 3.5mm crochet hook
- Fiberfill stuffing
- Two small safety eyes or embroidery thread
- Yarn needle
- Pipe cleaner (optional, for an arched tail or back pose)

## Abbreviations

- **MR** — magic ring
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **dec** — invisible decrease
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Head

- **Round 1:** MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
- **Round 2:** inc around (12)
- **Round 3:** (1 sc, inc) around (18)
- **Round 4:** (2 sc, inc) around (24)
- **Round 5:** (3 sc, inc) around (30)
- **Rounds 6–11:** sc around (30) — 6 rounds

Attach eyes between rounds 6-7, spaced about 8 stitches apart.

- **Round 12:** (3 sc, dec) around (24)
- **Round 13:** (2 sc, dec) around (18)

Stuff firmly.

- **Round 14:** (1 sc, dec) around (12)
- **Round 15:** dec around (6)

Fasten off, leaving a tail for sewing to the body.

## Ears (make 2)

- **Round 1:** MR, 4 sc in ring (4)
- **Round 2:** inc around (8)
- **Round 3:** sc around (8)

Fold flat and sew closed, then attach to the top of the head with the points facing slightly outward.

## Body

- **Round 1:** MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
- **Round 2:** inc around (12)
- **Round 3:** (1 sc, inc) around (18)
- **Rounds 4–8:** sc around (18) — 5 rounds
- **Round 9:** (1 sc, dec) around (12)

Stuff firmly.

- **Round 10:** dec around (6)

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Sew the body to the underside of the head.

## Legs (make 4)

- **Round 1:** MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
- **Rounds 2–5:** sc around (6) — 4 rounds

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Lightly stuff and sew two to the front of the body and two to the back, positioning the front legs slightly forward for a seated pose.

## Tail

Chain 12, then sc in 2nd chain from hook and across. If you want an arched, upright tail, insert a pipe cleaner into the crocheted tube before sewing the end closed, then bend into a curved shape before attaching to the back of the body.

## Finishing

Embroider a small pink nose and a simple curved mouth. Weave in all ends. Pose the tail and ears for a playful or spooky expression, depending on your preference.

*Tip: positioning the ears close together and slightly forward gives the cat a more alert, "Halloween spooked" expression, while spreading them apart gives a calmer look.*

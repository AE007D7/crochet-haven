This oversized beach tote uses an open mesh stitch that's fast to work up and naturally lets sand fall through — practical as well as stylish for a day at the beach or pool.

## Materials

- 250-300g worsted weight cotton yarn
- 5.0mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle
- Optional: wooden handles instead of crocheted straps

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **dc** — double crochet
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Base

Chain 20.

**Round 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across, 3 sc in last ch, continue sc down the other side, 2 sc in first st to close. (40 sc)

**Round 2:** inc in first and last st of each short end, sc around. (44)

**Rounds 3-4:** sc around, increasing at the rounded ends to keep flat. (approx. 50)

## Mesh Body

Switching to the open mesh pattern for the sides:

**Round 5 and onward:** *dc in next st, ch 1, skip 1 st*, repeat around, join with sl st to first dc.

**Following rounds:** ch 3 (counts as dc), *dc in next ch-1 space, ch 1, skip next dc*, repeat around, join.

Continue the mesh pattern for approximately 12-14 rounds, building the bag to about 14-16 inches tall.

## Top Edge

Work 2-3 rounds of solid sc around the top opening to stabilize the edge and prevent stretching.

## Handles

Chain a length for your desired handle size (typically 30-35 sts).

**Row 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across.

**Rows 2-3:** ch 1, sc across, for a sturdier strap.

Fasten off, leaving tails. Make a second handle to match.

## Assembly

Sew the ends of each handle to the inside top edge of the bag, spacing them evenly apart on the front and back.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. If desired, add a fabric lining with an inner pocket for keys and phone, since the mesh body itself is open-weave and won't contain small loose items.

*Tip: cotton yarn is essential here — it holds its shape when wet and dries quickly, unlike acrylic, which makes it ideal for actual beach use.*

This crossbody bag is a compact, practical everyday piece — big enough for a phone, cards, and keys, with a long adjustable strap for hands-free wear.

## Materials

- 200-250g worsted weight cotton yarn
- 4.0mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle
- A button or magnetic snap for the flap closure
- Optional: fabric for lining

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Bag Body

Worked in the round from the base up.

Chain 16.

**Round 1 (Base):** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across, 3 sc in last ch, continue sc down the other side, 2 sc in first st to close. (32 sc)

**Round 2:** inc in first and last st of each short end, sc around evenly. (36)

**Rounds 3-5:** sc around, increasing slightly at the rounded ends to keep the base flat. (approx. 40)

**Rounds 6-24:** sc around in each stitch with no increases, working in a continuous spiral. This builds the height of the bag to about 9-10 inches.

Fasten off at the top opening.

## Flap

Working from the back top edge of the bag, join yarn and work back and forth in rows:

**Row 1:** sc across the back stitches (about half the total round). Turn.

**Rows 2-8:** ch 1, sc across. Turn. — building a rectangular flap about 5-6 inches long.

**Row 9 (rounded edge, optional):** decrease 1-2 sts at each end for a few rows to taper the flap into a rounded or pointed shape, or leave straight for a simple rectangular flap.

Fasten off.

## Strap

Chain a length to your desired strap size (typically 120-140 sts for a crossbody length, adjust to preference). 

**Row 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across.

**Row 2:** ch 1, sc across for extra strength and thickness.

Fasten off, leaving tails at both ends for sewing.

## Assembly

Sew the ends of the strap securely to the inside top edges of the bag, on opposite sides. Sew or attach a button on the front of the bag body to align with a buttonhole or loop worked into the tip of the flap, or attach a magnetic snap between the flap and body.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. If lining, cut fabric to match the bag's interior shape and hand-sew it in place along the top edge.

*Tip: work the bag body in a tight gauge (go down half a hook size from what's recommended on your yarn label) for extra structure and to prevent stretching under the weight of contents.*

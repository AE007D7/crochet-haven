This lightweight blanket uses a soft, breathable shell stitch in baby-safe cotton, finishing at approximately 30x30 inches — the ideal size for swaddling a newborn or as a stroller blanket.

## Materials

- Soft DK weight cotton yarn (about 400-450g)
- 4.0mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **dc** — double crochet
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Gauge

Not critical for a blanket, but aim for a soft, slightly open fabric rather than a tight, stiff one for the best drape and breathability.

## Foundation

Chain a multiple of 6, plus 2 (for example, chain 122 for a 30-inch width).

## Shell Stitch Pattern

**Row 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook. *skip 2 ch, 5 dc in next ch, skip 2 ch, sc in next ch*. Repeat from * across. Turn.

**Row 2:** ch 1, sc in first st. *skip 2 sts, 5 dc in center dc of next shell, skip 2 sts, sc in next st*. Repeat from * across, ending sc in last st. Turn.

Repeat Row 2 until the blanket measures approximately 30 inches long, or your desired size.

## Border

Work 3 rounds of sc evenly around the entire outer edge, working 3 sc in each corner stitch to keep the blanket flat and square.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends securely — baby items get washed frequently, so extra-secure ends matter. Block gently and let dry flat.

*Tip: 100% cotton is the safest choice for anything that will be near a baby's face during swaddling — it's breathable, hypoallergenic for most babies, and holds up well to frequent washing.*

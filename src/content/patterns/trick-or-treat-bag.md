A sturdy, roomy bag for collecting Halloween candy, worked in a dense single crochet fabric with a simple applied pumpkin motif for seasonal flair.

## Materials

- Black or dark purple worsted weight cotton yarn (about 150g)
- Small amounts of orange and green yarn for the pumpkin motif
- 4.5mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Base

Chain 20.

**Round 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across, 3 sc in last ch, continue sc down the other side, 2 sc in first st to close. (40 sc)

**Round 2:** inc in first and last st of each short end, sc around. (44)

**Rounds 3-4:** sc around, increasing slightly at the rounded ends to keep the base flat. (approx. 48)

## Body

**Rounds 5-20:** sc around in each stitch with no increases, working in a continuous spiral. This builds the bag to about 10-11 inches tall.

Fasten off at the top opening.

## Pumpkin Motif

With orange yarn, MR, work 6 sc in ring, inc around, (1 sc, inc) around to about 18-24 sts depending on desired motif size, then fasten off and flatten into a circle. Add a small green stem shape and 2-3 surface-stitched vertical lines to suggest pumpkin ridges. Sew the finished motif to the front center of the bag.

## Handle

Chain a length for your desired handle size (typically 30-35 sts).

**Row 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across.

**Row 2:** ch 1, sc across for a sturdier strap.

Fasten off, leaving tails. Sew both ends to the inside top edge of the bag on opposite sides.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. Work 1 round of sc around the top opening if desired for a clean, reinforced edge.

*Tip: work the base and lower third of the bag with an extra strand of yarn held together for reinforcement — trick-or-treat bags take a lot of stress from a full night of candy collecting.*

This beanie uses the front-post/back-post double crochet combination to create a ribbed texture that hugs the head without a separate brim piece. It's one of the fastest satisfying makes in crochet.

## Materials

- 1 skein bulky weight yarn (approx. 100g / 150m)
- 6.5mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle
- Stitch marker

## Sizing

This pattern fits an average adult head (22"–23" circumference). To size down, chain fewer stitches in multiples of 2; to size up, add more.

## Pattern

Chain 46 (or adjust to your gauge/size — must be an even number), join with slip stitch, careful not to twist.

**Round 1:** Ch 2 (does not count as st), dc around (46 dc).

**Rounds 2–10 (Ribbing):** Ch 2, *FPdc around next st, BPdc around next st*, repeat around. This creates the ribbed look. Continue for about 6 inches of height.

**Decrease rounds (crown shaping):**

**Round 11:** *dc in next 4 sts, dec* — repeat around.
**Round 12:** dc around, no decrease.
**Round 13:** *dc in next 3 sts, dec* — repeat around.
**Round 14:** *dc in next 2 sts, dec* — repeat around.
**Round 15:** *dc in next st, dec* — repeat around.
**Round 16:** dec around.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Thread through remaining stitches and pull tight to close the top.

## Finishing

Weave in ends. Add a pom-pom to the top if desired, or leave it clean for a minimalist look.

*Tip: bulky yarn hides tension mistakes really well, which makes this a forgiving practice pattern for front-post/back-post stitches.*

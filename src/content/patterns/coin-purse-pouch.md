This small zippered pouch is one of the fastest useful projects in crochet — perfect for using up small amounts of leftover yarn, and genuinely handy for coins, earbuds, or lip balm.

## Materials

- Small amount of worsted weight yarn (about 20g)
- 4.0mm crochet hook
- A 4-5 inch zipper
- Sewing needle and thread (for attaching the zipper)
- Yarn needle

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Pouch Body

Worked as one flat rectangle, folded and seamed at the end.

Chain 16.

**Row 1:** sc in 2nd ch from hook and across. Turn. (15 sc)

**Rows 2-20:** ch 1, sc across. Turn.

This builds a rectangle approximately 4 inches wide and 5 inches tall (10 rows folds to make a 2.5 inch tall pouch — adjust row count for your desired finished size).

Fasten off.

## Assembly

Fold the rectangle in half widthwise, right sides together. Single crochet or whip stitch the two side seams closed, leaving the top edge open.

## Attaching the Zipper

Turn the pouch right-side out. Pin the zipper into the top opening, with the zipper teeth facing up and the fabric tape tucked slightly inside the opening on both sides. Using a sewing needle and thread, hand-sew the zipper tape securely to the inside of the top edge on both the front and back panels, using small whip stitches or a simple running stitch.

## Finishing

Weave in all yarn ends. Test the zipper to make sure it opens and closes smoothly before adding any final embellishments like a small tassel or bead on the zipper pull.

*Tip: single crochet fabric has some stretch, so sew the zipper in with the pouch opening held slightly stretched taut — this keeps the zipper lying flat rather than puckering once you let go.*

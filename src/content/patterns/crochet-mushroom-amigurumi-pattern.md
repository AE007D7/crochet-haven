> **Design guide:** This article explains construction and styling choices. It is not a complete stitch-by-stitch pattern; use a numbered pattern suited to your measurements when making the project.


A crochet mushroom has two distinct structures: a rounded cap and a narrower stem. The cap needs a stable underside so it does not flop over the stem. This assembly guide works with a mushroom pattern that supplies the shaping rounds and stitch counts.

## Make a Dense Sample

Choose a hook that creates firm fabric with the selected yarn. Stretch the sample gently: stuffing should not show through large openings. Keep a stitch marker at the start of each round and count after every increase or decrease round.

## Shape the Cap Separately

Follow the pattern's cap shaping and keep its edge even. If the design has separate top and underside pieces, test them together before stuffing. Add embroidered spots while the inside is accessible, securing their ends on the reverse.

## Support the Stem

Stuff the stem gradually in small amounts rather than forcing one large wad inside. The base should sit evenly if the mushroom is meant to stand. Compare the stem's top opening with its attachment point on the cap before closing either piece.

## Join Around the Whole Opening

Pin the cap in position and inspect the mushroom from all sides. Sew around the attachment area with matching yarn, taking consistent stitches through both pieces. A narrow join can act like a hinge and make the cap tilt.

For an item intended for a young child, embroider facial details and avoid detachable decorations or exposed supports. Inspect seams regularly. A decorative mushroom can be displayed beside a [crochet pumpkin](/patterns/crochet-pumpkin-home-decor), keeping small decorative pieces out of reach.

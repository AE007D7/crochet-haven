This friendly round-headed monster is an original, cartoonish design — big googly eyes, a wide stitched grin, and a single fuzzy horn, built for cuteness rather than scares.

## Materials

- Green worsted weight yarn (about 40g)
- Small amount of black yarn for facial details
- Small amount of white or cream yarn for the horn
- 3.5mm crochet hook
- Fiberfill stuffing
- Two large safety eyes (different sizes for a fun, googly look)
- Yarn needle

## Abbreviations

- **MR** — magic ring
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **dec** — invisible decrease
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Head and Body (worked as one piece)

1. MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. (1 sc, inc) around (18)
4. (2 sc, inc) around (24)
5. (3 sc, inc) around (30)
6–16. sc around, no increase (30) — 11 rounds

Attach the two safety eyes between rounds 7-8, spaced unevenly (one slightly higher than the other) for a playful, googly-eyed look.

17. (3 sc, dec) around (24)
18. (2 sc, dec) around (18)

Stuff firmly.

19. (1 sc, dec) around (12)
20. dec around (6)

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Weave through remaining stitches and cinch closed.

## Arms (make 2)

1. MR, 5 sc in ring (5)
2-8. sc around (5) — 7 rounds

Fasten off leaving a tail. Lightly stuff and sew to the upper sides of the body.

## Horn

With white or cream yarn:

1. MR, 5 sc in ring (5)
2-4. sc around (5) — 3 rounds

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Lightly stuff and sew to the top of the head, slightly off-center.

## Facial Details

Embroider a wide, curved stitched mouth in black yarn, adding a couple of small triangular teeth peeking out for extra character.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. Optionally brush the body gently with a wire pet brush for a soft, fuzzy monster texture.

*Tip: mismatched eye sizes and slightly asymmetrical features make this monster read as friendly and silly rather than symmetrical and serious — lean into the wonkiness.*

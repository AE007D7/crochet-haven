This friendly skeleton is built from simple round and tube shapes with embroidered bone lines, giving it a cheerful, cartoonish look rather than anything scary.

## Materials

- White or off-white worsted weight yarn (about 35g)
- Small amount of black yarn for facial features and joint details
- 3.5mm crochet hook
- Fiberfill stuffing
- Yarn needle

## Abbreviations

- **MR** — magic ring
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **dec** — invisible decrease
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Head

1. MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. (1 sc, inc) around (18)
4. (2 sc, inc) around (24)
5–9. sc around (24) — 5 rounds

Embroider two large black oval eye sockets and a simple triangular nose between rounds 5-6.

10. (2 sc, dec) around (18)
11. (1 sc, dec) around (12)

Stuff firmly.

12. dec around (6)

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Embroider a row of short horizontal stitches below the nose for the classic skeleton "teeth" smile.

## Body

1. MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. (1 sc, inc) around (18)
4-8. sc around (18) — 5 rounds
9. (1 sc, dec) around (12)

Stuff, then fasten off leaving a tail. Sew the head to the top of the body.

Embroider a row of curved "rib bone" lines across the front of the body using black yarn, in a stacked arch pattern.

## Arms and Legs (make 4 total)

1. MR, 5 sc in ring (5)
2-10. sc around (5) — 9 rounds

Fasten off leaving a tail. Lightly stuff. Embroider small evenly-spaced ring marks along the length of each limb to suggest joints. Sew two to the top sides of the body as arms and two to the bottom as legs.

## Hands and Feet (make 4)

1. MR, 5 sc in ring (5)
2. sc around (5)

Fasten off, stuff lightly, and sew to the ends of each limb.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. Add a small bow tie or hat for extra personality if desired.

*Tip: keep the embroidered "bone" lines simple and evenly spaced — a few clean curved stitches read more clearly as a ribcage than dense detailed lines.*

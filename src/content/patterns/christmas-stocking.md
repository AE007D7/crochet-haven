This roomy stocking works up in simple rows of half double crochet, with an optional textured cuff for contrast. It's fully functional — sturdy enough to hang and fill with small treats.

## Materials

- Red or green worsted weight yarn for the body (about 150g)
- Cream or white yarn for the cuff (about 50g)
- 5.0mm crochet hook
- Yarn needle
- Ribbon or cord for hanging loop

## Abbreviations

- **ch** — chain
- **sc** — single crochet
- **hdc** — half double crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **dec** — decrease
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Stocking Body (Make 2 Panels)

Chain 25.

**Row 1:** hdc in 3rd ch from hook and each ch across. Turn. (23 hdc)

**Rows 2-18:** Ch 2 (counts as first hdc), hdc across. Turn. (23 hdc each row)

This builds the leg portion of the stocking to about 12 inches. At row 18, begin shaping the foot:

**Row 19 (Heel Shaping):** Ch 2, hdc in next 15 sts, leave remaining sts unworked. Turn. (16 hdc)

**Rows 20-24:** Ch 2, hdc across the 16 sts. Turn. — 5 rows, building the heel.

**Row 25 (Toe Shaping):** Ch 2, hdc in next 5 sts, dec twice, hdc in remaining sts. Turn.

**Row 26:** Ch 2, hdc in next 3 sts, dec twice, hdc across. Turn.

Continue decreasing 2 stitches every row until 6-8 stitches remain, then fasten off, leaving a long tail.

Repeat entirely to make the second panel.

## Assembly

With right sides together (or wrong sides together for a visible seam look), whip stitch or single crochet the two panels together along the toe, heel, and side seam, leaving the top of the leg open.

## Cuff

With cream or white yarn, work a foundation chain matching the top opening width, then work 4-5 rows of hdc in a contrasting texture (front-post/back-post hdc for ribbing works well). Sew the cuff around the top opening, folding it over the outside of the stocking.

## Hanging Loop

Cut a length of ribbon or crochet a short chain, fold in half, and sew both ends to the inside back of the cuff to create a hanging loop.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. Block gently to even out the shape before hanging.

*Tip: work the two panels in slightly different shades of red if you want a subtle ombre look, or keep them identical for a clean, classic stocking.*

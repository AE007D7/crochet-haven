> **Design guide:** This article explains construction and styling choices. It is not a complete stitch-by-stitch pattern; use a numbered pattern suited to your measurements when making the project.


The 6-Day Star Blanket is a design by Betty McKnit. Use the [designer's original instructions](https://bettymcknit.com/patterns/6daystarblanket/) for the stitch sequence and shaping. This companion guide is about organizing yarn, tracking rounds and checking the growing star; it does not replace or reproduce that pattern.

## Plan a Palette You Can Continue

Lay the yarn colors beside one another in the order you want to use them. A light center and alternating darker bands can emphasize the radiating shape. Keep a written record of the color used in each round so you can continue consistently after a break.

Check the yarn labels for length as well as ball weight. Different yarns sold in the same weight category may have different yardage. If you change yarn, sample the fabric and check that the new strand behaves similarly before introducing it into the blanket.

## Give Every Point a Marker

Put a removable marker at each point as it becomes established, following the original pattern's structure. At the end of a round, compare the sections between points. If one section is shorter or fuller than the others, inspect that section before adding another round.

A star that waves or cups can reflect a missed shaping instruction, altered tension or an incompatible yarn change. Do not assume blocking will correct an incorrect stitch sequence.

## Manage the Growing Blanket

Support the blanket on your lap or a table while working. Its increasing weight can pull on the active stitches. Pause between color bands to spread it out gently and check the outline. Photographing it from above can reveal a lopsided point that is hard to see while it is folded.

## Finish Without Distorting the Shape

Follow the designer's finishing instructions and the yarn's care directions. Shape the points gently and avoid stretching only one section. The name is not a personal completion deadline: choose a schedule that fits your pace. For a simpler rectangle, see the [beginner blanket](/patterns/crochet-blanket-pattern-beginners).

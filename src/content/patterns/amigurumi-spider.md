This round-bodied spider is a fast, cute Halloween project — great scattered across a mantel, tucked into a [spiderweb coaster](/patterns/spiderweb-coasters), or hung from a doorway.

## Materials

- Black worsted weight yarn (about 15g)
- Small amount of white or red yarn for eyes/markings
- 4.0mm crochet hook
- Fiberfill stuffing
- Yarn needle
- Pipe cleaner pieces (optional, for posable legs)

## Abbreviations

- **MR** — magic ring
- **sc** — single crochet
- **inc** — increase
- **dec** — invisible decrease
- **ch** — chain
- **st(s)** — stitch(es)

## Body

1. MR, 6 sc in ring (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. (1 sc, inc) around (18)
4. (2 sc, inc) around (24)
5–8. sc around (24) — 4 rounds

Stuff firmly.

9. (2 sc, dec) around (18)
10. (1 sc, dec) around (12)
11. dec around (6)

Fasten off, leaving a tail. Weave through remaining stitches and cinch closed.

## Legs (make 8)

Chain 10, then sc in 2nd chain from hook and across. Fasten off leaving a tail at each end.

For posable legs, insert a short length of pipe cleaner into the crocheted tube before closing the end, allowing you to bend each leg into a realistic spider stance.

## Assembly

Sew four legs evenly spaced along each side of the body, angled slightly downward and outward.

## Eyes

With white yarn, embroider or attach a small cluster of tiny eyes on the front top of the body — spiders traditionally have 6-8 small eyes, but 2-4 simple dots reads clearly enough for a decorative piece.

## Finishing

Weave in all ends. Bend the legs into a natural crouching or climbing pose.

*Tip: for a fuzzy, more realistic spider texture, brush the body gently with a wire pet brush after stuffing — it raises the yarn fibers into a soft, fuzzy finish.*
